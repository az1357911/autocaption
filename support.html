常見問題

需要什麼環境？

Apple 晶片的 Mac、macOS 26 以上，以及 Mac 版 Final Cut Pro。智慧斷句需要開啟 Apple Intelligence（沒開時改用規則斷句）。

在 Final Cut Pro 裡找不到自動字幕？

下載後先打開一次「自動字幕」App，然後結束並重新打開 Final Cut Pro，再從選單「視窗 > 延伸功能 > 自動字幕」打開面板。

支援哪些語言？

語音辨識支援 macOS 提供的所有語言（依系統而定，目前約 45 種），台灣國語會轉成繁體中文與台灣用語。介面有 10 種語言。

免費版可以用多少？

沒有訂閱時，每個月可以免費處理 10 分鐘的音訊，每月 1 日重置。同一段重新辨識不會重複計算。訂閱後不限分鐘數。

怎麼取消或管理訂閱？

到「系統設定 > Apple 帳號 > 媒體與購買項目 > 訂閱」，或 App Store 的帳號頁面 >「管理訂閱」。免費試用要在結束前至少 24 小時取消才不會收費。退款請透過 Apple 的回報問題網站申請。

換了 Mac 或重新安裝，訂閱還在嗎？

在「自動字幕」App 的訂閱頁按「恢復購買」，用同一個 Apple 帳號即可恢復。

AI 翻譯會把我的字幕送到哪裡？

只有你在設定裡選用雲端 AI 服務、並填入自己的 API 金鑰時，字幕文字才會直接送到那家服務，第一次送出前會先問你。詳見隱私權政策。

FAQ

What do I need?

A Mac with Apple silicon, macOS 26 or later, and Final Cut Pro for Mac. Smart line breaking uses Apple Intelligence (rule-based line breaking is used when it’s off).

I can’t find AutoCaption in Final Cut Pro.

Open the AutoCaption app once after downloading, quit and reopen Final Cut Pro, then choose Window > Extensions > AutoCaption.

Which languages are supported?

Speech recognition supports every language macOS provides (about 45, depending on your system). The interface is available in 10 languages.

How much can I use for free?

Without a subscription you can process 10 minutes of audio per month, reset on the 1st. Re-recognizing the same clip doesn’t count twice. Subscribers have no limit.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Go to System Settings > Apple Account > Media & Purchases > Subscriptions, or your account page in the App Store > Manage Subscriptions. Cancel a free trial at least 24 hours before it ends to avoid being charged. For refunds, use Apple’s Report a Problem site.

I got a new Mac or reinstalled. Is my subscription still there?

Open the subscription page in the AutoCaption app and click Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account.

Where does AI translation send my subtitles?

Only if you select a cloud AI service and enter your own API key, the subtitle text is sent directly to that service, and the app asks you first. See the Privacy Policy.

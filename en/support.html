FAQ

What do I need?

A Mac with Apple silicon, macOS 26 or later, and Final Cut Pro for Mac. Smart segmentation requires Apple Intelligence to be turned on (when it’s off, rule-based segmentation is used instead).

Can’t find AutoCaption in Final Cut Pro?

After downloading, open the AutoCaption app once, then quit and reopen Final Cut Pro, and open the panel by choosing Window > Extensions > AutoCaption.

Which languages are supported?

Speech recognition supports all languages provided by macOS (depending on your system, currently about 45). Taiwanese Mandarin is converted to Traditional Chinese with Taiwan terms. The interface is available in 10 languages.

How much can I use for free?

Without a subscription, you can process 10 minutes of audio for free each month, reset on the 1st of every month. Recognizing the same section again doesn’t count twice. With a subscription, there’s no limit on minutes.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Go to System Settings > Apple Account > Media & Purchases > Subscriptions, or your account page in the App Store > Manage Subscriptions. To avoid being charged, cancel a free trial at least 24 hours before it ends. To request a refund, use Apple’s Report a Problem site.

I got a new Mac or reinstalled. Is my subscription still there?

On the subscription page in the AutoCaption app, click Restore Purchases and use the same Apple Account to restore it.

Where does AI translation send my subtitles?

Only when you choose a cloud AI service in Settings and enter your own API key is the subtitle text sent directly to that service, and the app asks you before sending it for the first time. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Summary: AutoCaption’s speech recognition and segmentation both happen on your Mac. The developer has no servers and does not collect, store, or have access to your videos, audio, subtitles, or any personal data. Subtitle text is sent to the service you chose only if you yourself choose cloud AI translation or AI correction, and the app asks you before sending it for the first time.

1. Data processed on your Mac

  • Video and audio: The app reads the media files referenced by the Final Cut Pro project you drag into the panel and converts the speech to text on your Mac using Apple’s speech recognition (SpeechAnalyzer). Media files are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Segmentation: Done on your Mac using Apple’s on-device Apple Intelligence model (Foundation Models).
  • Apple Translation: When you choose Apple Translation, translation is done on your Mac using the translation feature built into macOS.
  • Settings and Names & Terms list: Stored only on your Mac.
  • API keys: The AI service keys you enter are stored in the macOS Keychain and used only to connect directly to that service. They are not sent to the developer.
  • Diagnostic logs: The app may write brief activity logs on your Mac (file names only, not full paths). They are not sent to anyone automatically.

2. Data sent to third parties only when you choose

  • Cloud AI translation and AI correction: If you choose a cloud AI service in Settings (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google) and enter your own API key, the app sends the subtitle text, your Names & Terms list, and any notes you add for translation directly to the service you chose. Before sending for the first time, the app explains what will be sent and asks for your consent; you can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings > Translation. That service processes this data under its own privacy policy; please refer to that service’s terms. When you use a local service (Ollama, LM Studio), the data does not leave your computer.
  • AI model lists: When you use a cloud AI service and let the app choose the model automatically, the app uses your key to ask that service for its list of available models, and downloads the public model list and prices from OpenRouter (no account or key required, and no subtitle content or personal data included).
  • Speech and translation models: The first time you use a language, macOS downloads the corresponding speech recognition or translation model from Apple, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

3. Subscriptions and payment

Subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple’s App Store. Payment information is handled by Apple; the developer does not receive your name, payment method, or Apple Account information, only aggregated sales reports provided by Apple. The app checks your subscription status with the App Store on your Mac.

4. What the developer does not do

  • No analytics collection, no usage tracking, no ads.
  • No account required.
  • No selling or sharing of any personal data (the developer does not have your data in the first place).

5. Children

This app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Policy updates

When this policy changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will be revised; significant changes will be announced in the app’s release notes.

7. Contact

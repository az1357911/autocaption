隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 13 日・English version below

重點：自動字幕的語音辨識、斷句都在你的 Mac 上完成。開發者沒有伺服器，不收集、不儲存、也看不到你的影片、聲音、字幕或任何個人資料。只有在你自己選用雲端 AI 翻譯或 AI 校正時，字幕文字才會傳送到你選的那家服務，而且第一次傳送前會先問你。

1. 在你的 Mac 上處理的資料

  • 影片與聲音：App 讀取你拖進面板的 Final Cut Pro 專案所引用的媒體檔，在本機用 Apple 的語音辨識（SpeechAnalyzer）轉成文字。媒體檔不會上傳到任何地方。
  • 斷句：使用 Apple 裝置端的 Apple Intelligence 模型（Foundation Models），在本機完成。
  • Apple 翻譯：選用「Apple 翻譯」時，使用 macOS 內建的翻譯功能在本機完成。
  • 設定與專有名詞清單：只存在你的 Mac 上。
  • API 金鑰：你填入的 AI 服務金鑰存在 macOS 的鑰匙圈，只用來直接連線到那家服務，不會傳給開發者。
  • 診斷記錄：App 可能在你的 Mac 上寫入簡短的操作記錄（只記檔名，不記完整路徑），不會自動傳送給任何人。

2. 只有你選用時才會傳送到第三方的資料

  • 雲端 AI 翻譯與 AI 校正：如果你在設定裡選用雲端 AI 服務（例如 OpenAI、Anthropic、Google 等）並填入自己的 API 金鑰，App 會把字幕文字、專有名詞清單，以及你為翻譯填寫的補充說明直接傳送到你選的服務。第一次傳送前，App 會說明要傳送的內容並請你同意；你可以隨時在「設定 > 字幕翻譯」撤回同意。這些資料由該服務依其隱私權政策處理，請參閱該服務的條款。使用本機服務（Ollama、LM Studio）時，資料不會離開你的電腦。
  • AI 模型清單：使用雲端 AI 服務、讓 App 自動挑選模型時，App 會用你的金鑰向該服務查詢可用的模型清單，並向 OpenRouter 下載公開的模型清單與價格（不需要帳號或金鑰，不含任何字幕內容或個人資料）。
  • 語音與翻譯模型：第一次使用某種語言時，macOS 會從 Apple 下載對應的語音辨識或翻譯模型，由 Apple 依其隱私權政策處理。

3. 訂閱與付款

訂閱透過 Apple 的 App Store 購買與管理。付款資料由 Apple 處理，開發者不會取得你的姓名、付款方式或 Apple 帳號資料，只會收到 Apple 提供的彙總銷售報表。App 在你的 Mac 上向 App Store 確認訂閱狀態。

4. 開發者不做的事

  • 不收集分析數據、不做使用追蹤、不放廣告。
  • 不需要建立帳號。
  • 不出售、不分享任何個人資料（開發者本來就沒有你的資料）。

5. 兒童

本 App 不是針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會在知情的情況下收集兒童的個人資料。

6. 政策更新

政策有變動時會更新本頁並修改上方的生效日期；重大變動會在 App 更新說明中告知。

7. 聯絡方式

有任何隱私問題，請寫信到 az1357911+autocaption@gmail.com。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Summary: AutoCaption transcribes and segments speech on your Mac. The developer runs no servers and does not collect, store, or have access to your videos, audio, subtitles, or any personal data. Subtitle text leaves your Mac only if you choose a cloud AI service for translation or AI correction, and the app asks for your consent before the first time it sends anything.

1. Data processed on your Mac

  • Video and audio: The app reads the media files referenced by the Final Cut Pro project you drop into the panel and transcribes them on-device with Apple’s speech recognition (SpeechAnalyzer). Media files are never uploaded.
  • Line breaking: Done on-device with Apple Intelligence’s on-device model (Foundation Models).
  • Apple Translation: When you choose Apple Translation, translation runs on-device using macOS’s built-in translation.
  • Settings and glossary: Stored only on your Mac.
  • API keys: AI service keys you enter are stored in the macOS Keychain and used only to connect directly to that service. They are never sent to the developer.
  • Diagnostic logs: The app may write short activity logs on your Mac (file names only, not full paths). They are never sent automatically.

2. Data sent to third parties only when you choose

  • Cloud AI translation and AI correction: If you select a cloud AI service (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google) and enter your own API key, the app sends the subtitle text, your glossary, and any notes you add for the translation directly to that service. Before the first time, the app explains what will be sent and asks for your consent; you can withdraw consent at any time in Settings > Translation. That service processes the data under its own privacy policy. With local services (Ollama, LM Studio), data never leaves your computer.
  • AI model lists: When you use a cloud AI service with automatic model selection, the app uses your key to ask that service for its model list, and downloads the public model list and prices from OpenRouter (no account or key, no subtitle content or personal data).
  • Speech and translation models: The first time you use a language, macOS downloads the speech recognition or translation model from Apple, under Apple’s privacy policy.

3. Subscriptions and payment

Subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles payment; the developer never receives your name, payment method, or Apple Account details, only aggregated sales reports from Apple. The app checks your subscription status with the App Store on your Mac.

4. What the developer does not do

  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads.
  • No account required.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data (the developer does not have your data in the first place).

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the effective date above will be updated; significant changes will be noted in the app’s release notes.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, email az1357911+autocaption@gmail.com.
